title: "C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models"
authors: "Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He"

This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models, providing a standardized benchmark for evaluating the capabilities of Large Language Models in Chinese.

**Keywords:** Evaluation Benchmark, Chinese NLP, Foundation Models
