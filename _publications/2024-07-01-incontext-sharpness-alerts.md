title: "In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation"
authors: "Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He"
date: 2024-07-01

This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as alerts for hallucination mitigation, taking an inner representation perspective to identify and address hallucinations in Large Language Models.

**Keywords:** Hallucination Mitigation, Inner Representations, Large Language Models
